# Grammar Error Log

## 1. Frequent errors
| Error | Correct form | Why | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI have a positive impact | AI has a positive impact | Subject-verb agreement: singular subject + singular verb | AI has a positive impact on education. |
| a important role | an important role | Use "an" before a vowel sound | AI plays an important role in modern life. |
| people use computers | people used computers | Past tense is needed for a completed action in the past | In the past, people used computers mainly for work. |
| the shy student | shy students | Generic plural nouns do not usually take a definite article | Shy students often need more encouragement. |
| huge information | a huge amount of information | "Information" is uncountable, so use a quantity expression | A huge amount of information is available online. |
| in internet | on the internet | Use "on" as the preposition for the internet | You can search for information on the internet. |
| feel interest to some topic | feel interested in a topic | Use "be/feel interested in" for a hobby or subject | If you feel interested in a topic, you can study it. |
| may afraid | may be afraid | "Afraid" is an adjective, so it needs the verb "be" after modals | Shy students may be afraid to ask questions. |
| has an huge impact to | has a huge impact on | Use `impact on`, and use `a` before a consonant sound (`huge`) | Budget cuts have a huge impact on the elderly. |
| I am afraid of I can't help you | I'm afraid I can't help you | `afraid of` takes a noun / V-ing. Bad news uses `I'm afraid (that) + clause` | I'm afraid I can't join the meeting today. |
| I am familiar with setup RAG | I am familiar with setting up RAG | `be familiar with` is followed by a noun or V-ing, not a bare verb | After 100 days of training, I am familiar with setting up RAG. |
| Practice make / he specialize | Practice makes / he specializes | Singular subjects take a singular verb with `-s` | Practice makes perfect. He specializes in robotics. |
| talk anything on the internet | talk about anything on the internet | Use `talk about + topic` with `on the internet` | You can talk about anything on the internet. |
| Budget cuts has a huge impact | Budget cuts have a huge impact | Check the subject first. Plural subjects take `have`, even with the chunk `have/has an impact on` | Budget cuts have a huge impact on public hospitals. |
| familiar with to use | familiar with using | `be familiar with` takes a noun or V-ing, not `to + verb` | After two weeks, I am familiar with using this software. |
| may not happy | may not be happy | After `may / can / will / should`, adjectives still need `be` | You may not be happy with your looks. |
| don't makes sense | doesn't make sense | Singular subject takes `doesn't`. After `doesn't`, use the base verb form | This financial model doesn't make sense to me. |
| in my team | on my team | Preposition: use "on" when referring to membership in a team | There was no senior developer on my team. |
| in the further | in the future | Word form: "future" is a noun (time to come); "further" is an adjective/adverb (more distant) | This would help me make technical decisions in the future. |
| No senior on my team | There was no senior on my team | Use the "there was / there were" structure to state existence in the past | There was no senior on my team to guide me. |

## 2. Reflection
- Be careful with singular/plural agreement in scientific and academic writing.
- Use articles based on sound, not just spelling.
- Check whether the sentence describes a past fact, a general truth, or a specific example.
- For uncountable nouns like information, use quantity phrases such as "a large amount of" or "a great deal of".
- Keep `have an impact on`, not `impact to`.
- Do not mix `afraid of + noun / V-ing` with `I'm afraid that + clause`.
- After `be familiar with`, use a noun or V-ing, not `to + verb`.
- After a modal verb, do not drop `be` before an adjective.

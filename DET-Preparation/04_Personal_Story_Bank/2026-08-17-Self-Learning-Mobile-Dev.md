# Personal Story Bank

## Theme
Self-learning experience / Software or technology background

## Prompt
Describe a time when you had to learn a new technology, tool, or skill on your own.

## Conditions
- Date: 2026-08-17 (Daily Check-in)
- Target: 3–5 sentences
- Context: WorldQuant University (WQU) MSc Prep

## My Original Version
Few years ago , when I just be a mobile developer , and my first job was submitted the apps to ios and android. No senior in my team and I have to figure out how to complete my task. I researched a huge amount of content and I trained my self to make a decision table to put all useful and updated information , it would help me in the further when I make technical decision.

## Refined Version
A few years ago, when I was just a mobile developer, my first job was to submit apps to iOS and Android. There was no senior on my team, so I had to figure out how to complete my tasks on my own. I researched a huge amount of information and trained myself to create a decision table to organize all the useful and updated information, which would help me in the future when making technical decisions.

## Stronger DET Version (WQU Admissions Style)
A few years ago, early in my career as a mobile developer, my primary responsibility was deploying applications to iOS and Android platforms. Since there were no senior developers on my team to guide me, I had to independently figure out how to accomplish these tasks. To manage this challenge, I researched a huge amount of technical documentation and designed a structured decision table to consolidate all updated information. This proactive approach significantly enhanced my ability to make sound technical decisions in the future.

## Key Improvements
- Few years ago ➔ A few years ago (need "a" before "few")
- when I just be ➔ when I was just / early in my career as
- ios and android ➔ iOS and Android (proper noun capitalization)
- No senior in my team ➔ There was no senior on my team (added "There was" and corrected "in" to "on")
- I have to figure out ➔ I had to figure out (corrected past tense)
- trained my self ➔ trained myself (single word)
- make a decision table ➔ create/design a decision table (more professional verb)
- put all useful... information ➔ organize/consolidate all... information
- , it would help me ➔ , which would help me / This would help me (avoided comma splice)
- in the further ➔ in the future (corrected word form confusion)
- when I make technical decision ➔ when making technical decisions / when I had to make technical decisions (plural noun and consistent past tense)

## Useful Patterns
- a few years ago (introducing past stories)
- there is/was no + noun
- on my team (correct preposition)
- train myself to + verb
- in the future
- make technical decisions

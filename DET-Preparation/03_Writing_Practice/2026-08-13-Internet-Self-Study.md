# Writing Practice (Before / After)

## Prompt
What is one advantage of using the internet to study a new subject (such as finance or programming) on your own?

## Conditions
- Time: Review Session Quiz
- Dictionary / AI: No
- Target: 3–5 sentences

## My Original Version
You can learn new things anytime , anywhere in internet. If you feel interest to some topic , you can search and study independently. With proper guidance and effective learning approach , you can be a quick learner and that motivate you to specialize something you interest

## Refined Version
You can learn new things anytime and anywhere on the internet. If you are interested in a certain topic, you can search and study independently. With proper guidance and an effective learning approach, you can be a quick learner, and that motivates you to specialize in something you are interested in.

## git  (Advanced Vocabulary & Flow)
The internet enables individuals to acquire new knowledge anytime and anywhere. If you develop an interest in a specific subject, you can easily conduct research and study independently. Guided by an effective learning strategy, you can accelerate your learning curve, which ultimately motivates you to specialize in a field you are passionate about.

## Key Improvements
- anytime , anywhere ➔ anytime and anywhere
- in internet ➔ on the internet
- feel interest to some topic ➔ are interested in a certain topic
- effective learning approach ➔ an effective learning approach
- that motivate ➔ that motivates
- specialize something ➔ specialize in something
- something you interest ➔ something you are interested in

## Useful Patterns
- on the internet
- be interested in + noun / V-ing
- feel an interest in + noun
- specialize in + noun
- motivate someone to + verb

## My Takeaway
I need to pay close attention to prepositions (on the internet, specialize in) and the "interest" adjective/verb forms (be interested in). Also, I must ensure singular countable nouns like "approach" have an article ("an approach"), and singular pronouns like "that" agree with singular verbs ("motivates").

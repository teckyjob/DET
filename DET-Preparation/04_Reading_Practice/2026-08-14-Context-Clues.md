# Reading Practice — Context Clues & Word Class

## Date
2026-08-14

## Goal
Use context clues to infer meaning and identify word class from sentence structure.

## Practice 1 — reluctant

### Sentence
Although Maya was initially **reluctant** to speak during meetings, she gradually became more confident after her manager encouraged her to share her ideas.

### My Answers
- Meaning: **B. unwilling or hesitant**
- Context clue: `after her manager encouraged her to share her ideas`
- Word class: adjective

### Why
`reluctant` describes Maya's state after the linking verb `was`.

Pattern:
- `Maya was reluctant.`
- `S + be + adjective`

## Practice 2 — ambiguous

### Sentence
The instructions were so **ambiguous** that several students interpreted them in different ways.

### My Answers
- Meaning: **B. confusing or open to more than one meaning**
- Context clue: `students interpreted them in different ways`
- Original word-class answer: verb
- Correct word class: **adjective**

### Why
The sentence structure is:

`The instructions` + `were` + `ambiguous`

So `were` is the verb, while `ambiguous` describes the instructions.

Pattern:
- `The instructions were ambiguous.`
- `The question was difficult.`
- `The explanation was confusing.`
- `S + be + adjective`

## Current Assessment

### Strength
- Context-clue inference is good.
- Can infer unfamiliar vocabulary from consequences and surrounding meaning.

### Priority
- Word-class identification is not yet stable.
- Pay special attention to `S + be + adjective` before assuming that an unfamiliar word is a verb.

## Next Review Rule
When reviewing this topic, do not reveal the answer immediately. Give a new sentence with an unfamiliar or semi-familiar word and ask:

1. What does the word probably mean?
2. Which words in the sentence are the context clues?
3. Is it a noun, verb, adjective, or adverb? Why?
